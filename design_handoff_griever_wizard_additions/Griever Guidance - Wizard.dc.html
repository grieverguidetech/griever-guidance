The person using this is exhausted, on their phone, and grieving. Every screen below has one job and one primary action — large touch targets, short copy, nothing to configure. The landing screen opens a session: their loved one's name and date are asked once and carried into every message, so the flows that follow ask for almost nothing. If a message is already in progress, the landing screen offers to continue it instead.
